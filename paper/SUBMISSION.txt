L4DC 2027 submission preparation

All manuscript text, proofs, appendices and references remain in main.tex.
The official l4dc2027.cls is included alongside the existing jmlr.cls.

Rebuild the complete PDF and submission pair from this directory:

    sh compile_paper.sh

The PDF split requires pypdf. The build script detects a suitable local Python;
otherwise install pypdf in your Python environment or set L4DC_PDF_PYTHON.
To split an already compiled complete PDF:

    python3 prepare_submission.py

Outputs:
    output/pdf/paper_complete.pdf      Complete paper, references and supplement
    output/pdf/paper_main.pdf          Main paper and references
    output/pdf/paper_supplement.pdf    Supplementary proofs and Lean references
    output/pdf/submission_manifest.json    Length, hashes and cross-part links

he performed this work. Enoch's affiliation is UCSB Mechanical Engineering.
