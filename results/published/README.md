# Published results

These are the saved numerical reports for the manuscript's original frozen
predictors. They contain no trained weights. The reserved-case CSV reports every
signed pulse, including numerical growth stops. Order errors and certificate
summaries supply the figure's numerical context.

Fresh training writes separate reports under `runs/`; it does not overwrite
these publication records. The full frozen-model audit and checkpoints remain
in the local dissertation archive and audit package.
